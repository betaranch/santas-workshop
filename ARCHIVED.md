## What Was This Project?

The **Elf Speakeasy Pop-Up** was a planned immersive holiday experience for Bend, Oregon, scheduled to run November 1, 2025 through January 1, 2026.

The concept combined:
- **Hospitality**: Craft cocktails in an adult-focused evening experience
- **Theater**: Character-driven storytelling with elves as hosts
- **Experiential Design**: "Portal, not pop-up" immersive environments

## Why Archived?

This repository has been archived and is now read-only. The project planning phase has concluded.
